HOW TO READ A CARD

1 2 3 4 5 6
1Status dot. Green: all clear. Orange: something to do. Red: CI is broken or there is a security alert.
2The facts. Latest release, CI on the default branch, last push, stars and forks.
3Open pull requests with the state of their checks. Click a row to open it.
4Open issues with labels and age. Long lists fold behind “Show more”.
5What to do next, most urgent first. A clear repo just says “Nothing to do”.
6Who can see it. “live” and a green border: public. “private” and a blue border: not public.
